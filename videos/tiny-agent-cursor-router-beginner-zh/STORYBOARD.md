## Video direction

- **Visual system:** warm paper `#ECECEA`, ink `#111111`, active blue `#0A84FF`; caution red `#FF5A4F` appears only for warnings. Keep the pale-blue graph grid and the top/bottom hairlines on every frame.
- **Typography:** Chinese display and body text use the render-safe generic `sans-serif` family; Latin product names use the same stack. Hierarchy comes from size and spacing, not decorative fonts.
- **Portrait composition:** keep all load-bearing content within the upper 74% of the 1080×1920 canvas. Reserve `y ≥ 1430` for embedded captions and Douyin UI; no critical label, character face, or CTA may enter that band.
- **Character system:** only one Tiny Agent appears in each frame, approximately 230–300 px wide, supporting the product idea instead of becoming the subject. No human presenter, additional mascot, or long-video chapter furniture.
- **Motion rhythm:** a quick hook, a clear mechanism reveal, a slightly denser three-mode build, one caution beat, then a calm CTA hold. Reveals follow the voiceover with long-tail `power3` easing; no front-loaded burst and no screensaver-style floating.
- **Negative constraints:** no generic AI gradient, browser chrome, fabricated product UI, photoreal human, decorative dashboard, rounded-glass cards, or model-performance claims beyond the cited official update.
- **Caption plan:** phrase-level Chinese captions, maximum two lines, centered above the reserved lower band. Captions reuse the Tiny Agent voice and subtitle system but do not reuse the longform chapter/recap layout.
