## Verified facts used in this episode

- Long interaction logs can mix relevant experience with low-value detail, making retrieval slower and less reliable.
- PlugMem converts interactions such as dialogues, documents, and web sessions into structured knowledge units: facts and reusable skills.
- Its three components are structured transformation, task-aligned retrieval, and concise task-ready reasoning guidance.
- Microsoft Research evaluated the same module on long multi-turn conversations, multi-article fact finding, and web browsing decisions; the blog reports better performance with fewer memory tokens than the compared generic and task-specific designs.
- These findings are benchmark evidence, not a guarantee for every production agent. The episode's retain-distill-forget table is an applied interpretation, not a claim from the research.
